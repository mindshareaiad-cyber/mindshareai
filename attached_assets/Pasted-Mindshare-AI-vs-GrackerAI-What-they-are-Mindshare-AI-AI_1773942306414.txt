Mindshare AI vs GrackerAI
What they are

Mindshare AI: AI visibility and AEO platform that shows how AI assistants talk about your brand, gives you scores, competitor benchmarks, and a clear playbook (plus API) to improve visibility.

GrackerAI: GEO + AI visibility platform for B2B SaaS that tracks citations and auto‑builds GEO‑optimised content hubs, comparison pages, and pSEO portals.

Key differences

Execution:

Mindshare: Measures and advises; your team or agency executes the strategy.

Gracker: Measures and executes, auto‑publishing content to win citations.

Buyer fit:

Mindshare: Best for SaaS marketers and agencies with existing content/SEO muscle who want a “radar + playbook” and flexible workflows.

Gracker: Best for B2B SaaS wanting a semi‑done‑for‑you GEO system that ships portals and comparison pages on autopilot.

Pricing/feel:

Mindshare: Product‑led, API‑friendly, easier to plug into stacks.

Gracker: More sales‑led, programmatic engagement with case‑study heavy value prop.

Mindshare AI vs Peec AI
What they are

Peec AI: AI search analytics tool focused on monitoring brand visibility across ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, and AI Overviews—clean dashboards, citation tracking, competitor SOV, and source analysis.

Mindshare AI: Also tracks multi‑engine AI visibility and SOV, but is built from day one with an AEO improvement playbook and agency‑friendly workflows.

Key differences

Depth vs direction:

Peec: Excellent monitoring—visibility %, citations, source domains, multi‑engine SOV—but widely described as “data without direction” (great diagnosis, weak on prescriptions).
​

Mindshare: Emphasises actionability—weekly actions, AEO recommendations, and concrete prompts/keywords to target, not just dashboards.

ICP:

Peec: Good for teams that already know AEO and just need analytics; suited to experienced in‑house SEO / content leads.

Mindshare: Better for SaaS marketers and agencies who want both measurement and a clear playbook they can sell into stakeholders.

Extensibility:

Peec: Offers exports and some integrations; API is more enterprise‑gated.

Mindshare: Public API and “Am I on AI?” style flows aimed at custom dashboards, multi‑client use, and embedding into existing reporting.

Mindshare AI vs Scrunch AI
What they are

Scrunch AI: AEO / GEO platform with a strong Agent Experience Platform (AXP)—it builds AI‑optimised parallel versions of your pages, adds technical fixes at the edge, and focuses on how AI agents crawl, render, and cite your site.

Mindshare AI: Focuses on what AI assistants actually say about you (answers, prompts, SOV, sentiment) and how to improve that through AEO content and distribution—not on deep edge‑side technical optimisation.

Key differences

Orientation:

Mindshare: Content and visibility oriented—prompts, answers, share of voice, brand perception—ideal for marketers.

Scrunch: Very technical—AXP, agent‑friendly mirrors, real‑time technical error detection, SOC2, RBAC.

Coverage:

Scrunch: Widest engine coverage at its tier (ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity, Gemini, Google AI Overviews, AI Mode, Copilot, Meta AI).

Mindshare: Multi‑engine coverage focused on the main assistants you care about for SaaS (positioned more as AI assistants generally).

Best fit:

Mindshare: SaaS brands and agencies who want clarity on AI visibility + actions, without ripping up infrastructure.

Scrunch: Enterprises with complex, global sites and strong security needs who need deep technical AEO and an AI‑optimised parallel site layer.
